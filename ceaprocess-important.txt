2) PRIMARY WORKFLOW ( /process ) — Account Distribution
============================================================
Step 1. Save Your List (toggle: “Show steps”)
- Open PMAC in CEW
- Find the Resources tab and open CART (Client Account Redistribution Tool)
- Find the Domain ID that was assigned the accounts in the top left. If none show up under your FA ID, go to Finder → Domain and ensure new Producer IDs are selected
- Go to Distribution Detail
- Set the start/end period to ensure you download the appropriate distributions
- On the right side of the page, click the green X page to download the current table as an excel sheet

Step 2. Split accounts in team

Step 3. Assign Action Plan CAP to accounts
- Can assign all at once
- Only click save once
Best practices (toggle):
- To assign all at once: sort the list of people you need to assign alphabetically in Excel
- Scroll through the All Clients page on Salesforce and select each person
- Click Create Action Plans (top right)

Step 4. Prep outreach
- Check Finder to view holdings
- Check New Holdings View to see annuities / PE / hedge funds / alts
- Determine if Low-Bal Close-Out, Maintain Pool, or CI/IAP

Step 5. Decision: choose path
Buttons:
- 5a. Low Balance Close-Out
- 5b. Maintain Pool
- 5c. Consumer Investments / IAP

Branch 5a → Step 6. Low Balance Close-Out
- Submit form to OMT
- Request OMT for automatic approval (<$15)
- Final outcome update
- Use OMT escalation form if needed
Link: /resources/omt-escalation

Branch 5b → Step 6. Maintain Pool
- Outreach to client to inform account realignment
- Document calls on Salesforce
- Follow-up email on realignment
Special cases (alts/exceptions):
- Use when you see alternative investments (annuities, PE, hedge funds, alts) or other exceptions.
Link: /managing-special-cases

Branch 5c → Step 6. Consumer Investments / IAP
- Letter of Authorization (eSign)
- Transfer to appropriate solution

Per outbound attempt — Step 6
- Update Action Plan item(s)
- Log call in Salesforce notes

Step 7
- Assign CEA (3–8 outbounds) if no final outcome by 2nd outreach

3) SPECIAL CASES ( /managing-special-cases )
============================================================
Alternative investments (quick guidance)
- If you see annuities, private equity, hedge funds, or other alternative holdings, treat the case as a special workflow.
- Goal: avoid promising timelines and route to correct internal process.

What to check
- Is the asset transferable or does it require liquidation?
- Any surrender charges / lockups / notice periods?
- Who is the product sponsor / platform?
- Is there a required LOA / additional forms?

What to say
- Set expectations: “We’re confirming requirements and next steps.”
- Avoid guarantees: don’t promise a timeline.
- Document: note the holdings + what you routed to.

Next steps
1) Confirm holdings in Finder + New Holdings View.
2) Collect required details (product type, sponsor, restrictions).
3) Route to the correct internal team/process (TBD).
4) Update Salesforce notes + action plan items.

6) HSA GUIDELINES ( /resources/hsa )
============================================================
Note
- Merrill Lynch no longer offers HSAs.
- Clients can open Bank of America (BofA) Health Benefit Solutions HSA.

Annual limits
- $4,400 single
- $8,750 family
- Age 55+: +$1,000
- 55 as of 12/13 of current calendar year

Monthly limits
- Annual contribution limit divided into 12 monthly amounts.
- For each month with an HDHP (as of first of month), you can contribute that monthly amount.

Fees
- $50 annual custodial fee
- $50 closeout/transfer out fee

Withdrawals/distributions
- HSA/MSA Distribution Form

Contributions into Merrill HSAs
- Check or Journal Entry (JE)
- FTS and Direct Deposits (DDS) no longer accepted

Transfers into Merrill HSAs
- Not allowed
- Clients can transfer into a BofA HSA

Transfers out of Merrill
- Eligible via ATS Online (ACAT & Non-ACAT)
- Must be fully liquidated to cash (no in-kind via ATS Online)
- ACAT: gaining firm initiates per NYSE Rule 412
- Non-ACAT: client establishes contra account; paperwork; contra firm mails to branch; submit via ATS Online

Transfer Merrill HSA → BofA HSA
Opening a BofA HSA account
- Apply online or call 1.800.992.3200
  - Option #1 Individual Accounts
  - Option #2 Group Accounts
  - 9am–6pm Mon–Fri EST
Existing BofA HSA assistance / fee waiver
- 1.866.791.0250 (8am–11pm EST)
Online access
- MyHealth BofA app → New User setup
Desktop access
- If employer provided an enrollment code: use “I have a code”
- If not: “Get Started”
